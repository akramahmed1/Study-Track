# StudyTrack – Student Guide

StudyTrack is one place to keep your assignments, due dates and reminders. There is no sign-up and no password.

## Before you start: where your records are kept

**Your records are stored only in this browser, on this device.** They are not sent anywhere.

- If you clear your browser data, your records are gone.
- If you open StudyTrack on a different device or in a different browser, you will not see them.
- On a shared or school computer, use **Settings → Clear all StudyTrack data** when you finish.

Always rely on your official course deadlines. StudyTrack is a helper, not the official record.

## Try it with sample data

Open **Settings** and press **Load sample assignments**. This adds three courses and eight example assignments so you can practise. When you are done, press **Clear all StudyTrack data** to remove them.

## Add a course

1. On the dashboard, find **Courses**.
2. Type the course name (for example "Biology 101") and press **Add course**.

Each course card has **Rename** and **Delete** buttons.

- **Rename:** change the name and press **Save**.
- **Delete:** you can only delete a course that has no assignments. If it still has assignments, StudyTrack tells you and does not delete it. Delete or move those assignments first. You are always asked to confirm before anything is deleted.

## Add an assignment

1. Press **New assignment**.
2. Fill in:
   - **Title** (required)
   - **Course** (required). Add a course first if the list is empty.
   - **Due date and time** (required)
   - **Reminder date and time** (optional)
   - **Notes** (optional)
3. Press **Save assignment**.

If something is missing or wrong, the form stays open and a message appears next to the field. Nothing is saved until the form is correct.

## Reminders

- A reminder appears as a box at the top of the page with the assignment name and due date. Press **Dismiss** to close it, or **View** to open the assignment.
- **Reminders only work while StudyTrack is open in your browser.** StudyTrack checks every 30 seconds. If the page is closed, or the reminder time passed while it was closed, you will not get a reminder.
- The reminder time must be at or before the due time. Otherwise StudyTrack asks you to fix it.
- If you change the reminder, the new time is used. If you complete or delete the assignment, its reminder is cancelled.

## See and open assignments

The dashboard shows how many assignments are **Upcoming**, **Overdue** and **Completed**. The list below is sorted with the soonest due date first. Press an assignment to open its details, where you can **Edit**, **Mark complete** (or **Reopen**) and **Delete**.

An assignment is:

- **Completed** when you have marked it complete.
- **Overdue** when it is not completed and its due time has passed. It is marked with the word "Overdue" and a warning symbol.
- **Upcoming** otherwise.

## Complete and reopen

Open an assignment and press **Mark complete**. Changed your mind? Open it again and press **Reopen**.

## Filters

Above the list, choose **All**, **Upcoming**, **Overdue** or **Completed**. Use **Filter by course** to show one course only. The two filters work together, for example "Overdue" in "Calculus I".

## Course progress

Each course shows how many of its assignments are completed, such as "1 of 3 completed (33%)". StudyTrack works this out from your records, so it is always up to date.

## Your name

In **Settings**, type a name and press **Save name**. The header then says "Hi, your name". It is only a label, not an account.

## Keyboard tips

Everything works with the keyboard. **Tab** moves forward, **Shift+Tab** back, **Enter** or **Space** presses a button, and **Escape** closes a confirmation box. A "Skip to main content" link appears when you first press Tab.

## If you see a warning

- **"Changes could not be saved":** your browser would not let StudyTrack save. StudyTrack stops saving to protect what is already there. Copy down anything important and check your browser's storage or privacy settings.
- **"Your saved StudyTrack data could not be read":** the stored data is damaged. Nothing was changed. You can choose **Clear saved data** to start fresh.
